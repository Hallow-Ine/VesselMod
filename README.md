### SOULS and FLESH:
SOULs are the culmination of one's will. <br />
FLESH is the mean by which will is conducted <br />

SOULs, under the right circumstances, can be placed under CONTRACT.
In that way, the will of a person can be passed down to another for a price that is decided by the bearer of the SOUL.

FLESH with no SOUL becomes stale, weakened and incapable of expressing any will, we call it a VESSEL.


### CARDINALS:
Beyond FLESH and beyond SOULS lie entities capable of miracles.

SOUL bearers are capable of interacting with these entities through ALTARS, structures achoring our world to theirs.

Should the entity that a bearer decided to commune with find interest in what they have to offer, one might find themselves covered in power, wealth or more.


### CONTRACTS:
Holding the very essence of a bearer, their SOUL, CONTRACTS exist for anyone to commune with others or entities beyond reason.

A bearer may infuse their very will into a blank CONTRACT, ready to be remodeled into a demonstration of belonging by anyone who it may be shared with.
Contracting one's SOUL however results in the separation of FLESH and SOUL which weakens the bonds of the form.
